A distance sensor measures the distance, in **metres**, to the first object in front of it, up to 0.8 m. The starter robot has three: `leftDist`, `frontDist` and `rightDist`, looking left, forward and right.

## Finding walls

A tile is 12 cm wide, so when the robot is in the middle of a tile, a wall on its side is about 5 cm away. This program checks the three sensors, and prints which ones see a wall:

<CodeTabs file="walls" />

Sensors measure from where they are on the robot, not from its centre. Print the values next to a wall to choose your own limits.
