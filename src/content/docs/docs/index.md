Erebus is a robot simulation competition environment. You design a robot, and program it to autonomously explore a maze, find and identify wall tokens, avoid obstacles and traps, keep its battery charged, and build a map of the environment.

This documentation covers **Erebus v27**, for the **RoboCupJunior Rescue Simulation 2027** rules. See [Versions](/docs/reference/versions/) for the other supported versions.

## The learning path
Follow the steps in order. Each one builds on the one before.

| Step | Pages | You will... |
|---|---|---|
| **Start** | [What is Erebus?](/docs/introduction/), [Installation](/docs/installation/) | Know what you are doing, and have everything installed |
| **1. The game** | [Rules summary](/docs/rules/) | Know the mission, the field and how to score |
| **2. Your robot** | [Get your robot](/docs/robot/), [Build your robot](/docs/robot/build/) | Have the starter robot loaded in Erebus |
| **3. First run** | [Your first run](/docs/getting-started/) | Watch your robot explore, and know the control window |
| **4. Programming basics** | [Your first program](/docs/programming/first-program/), [Movement](/docs/programming/movement/), [Sensors](/docs/programming/sensors/) | Make your robot move and sense |
| **5. Play the game** | [Navigate the maze](/docs/programming/navigate-the-maze/), [Communication](/docs/programming/communication/), [Wall Tokens](/docs/programming/wall-tokens/), [Battery](/docs/programming/battery/), [Exploring and mapping](/docs/programming/exploring/), [Mapping rules](/docs/rules/mapping/), [Submitting the map](/docs/programming/map-bonus/) | Score points in every way |
| **6. Compete** | [Preparing for a competition](/docs/competition/), [Remote Controller](/docs/competition/remote-controller/) | Be ready for a competition |

Every example uses the [starter robot](/docs/robot/), so load it before running them.
